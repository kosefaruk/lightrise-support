Common questions

How do I move and use my light?

Drag on the left side to move. Use the right-side buttons to jump, use light, strike with the staff and flash. Light toggles on and off by default. Find control options in Settings.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Gameplay works offline and does not require an account or in-app purchases. The game is supported by ads between some completed levels. Ads and advertising privacy choices require an internet connection; unavailable ads never block progression.

How do I manage advertising privacy?

Google's consent form appears where required. Settings shows Ad Privacy Choices when that option is required and available. You can separately change iOS tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking does not prevent you from playing.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. New Game resets progress while keeping settings. Deleting the app removes local app data; Apple's device backups may retain or restore a copy according to your settings.

How can I change the controls or language?

Open Settings from the main menu or pause menu. You can adjust button size and opacity, choose a left-handed layout, change light mode, and select English or Turkish.
