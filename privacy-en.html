Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026
Developer: Faruk Kose
Contact: farukkose19@gmail.com

Lightrise is a free game supported by Google AdMob advertising between some completed levels. The game does not require an account. Gameplay can work offline; loading ads and updating advertising privacy choices require an internet connection.

Saved on your device

The game stores progress, checkpoint information, collected items and settings (such as language, audio and controls) locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs. These records are used only to save your game and preferences. They are not sent to our servers; we do not operate a game backend.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until they are replaced by gameplay, reset or removed with the app. New Game resets game progress; settings remain. Deleting the app from iOS Settings removes its local app data. Apple's device backup and restore features may retain or restore a copy according to your Apple settings.

Advertising and information processed by Google

Lightrise uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your consent, device settings and the ad being served, Google and its advertising partners may process IP address (including approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ads viewed or clicked, app interactions, crash reports and performance diagnostics. These data may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, provide security and improve advertising services. We do not operate our own game-account or analytics backend. Google's data handling and retention are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Your advertising choices

Where required, Google's consent form is shown before ads can be requested. You can reopen the form through Settings > Ad Privacy Choices when that option is required and available. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt separately controls permission to access the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change that permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining tracking does not prevent you from playing. Ads may still be shown without that identifier, according to your consent and applicable settings. Changing your choices affects future ad requests; Google explains retention and deletion options in its policies.

Permissions and purchases

Lightrise does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or photos. The current version has no in-app purchases or social login. Advertising is optional to the operation of the game: if consent is unavailable or an ad cannot load, gameplay continues.

Children

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information directly. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about your child's use of Lightrise or advertising privacy, contact us. Google's advertising and consent practices are covered by the policies linked above and by the choices available on the device.

Support messages

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents you send. We use them to answer your request and resolve the issue, and delete them when no longer needed unless retention is legally required. These messages are not collected automatically by the game. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. You can read it from Settings at any time. For questions or requests about support correspondence, contact farukkose19@gmail.com.
